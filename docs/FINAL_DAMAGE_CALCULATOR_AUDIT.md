# Final Damage Calculator Audit — Current Implementation

Updated: 2026-10-17, after the Earthquake eligibility correction and final requested corrections. Scope: the NEW Steps2–6C direct-damage calculator plus Step8 presentation. Step7 KO prediction was intentionally skipped.

## Current result

- **162 eligible reference moves, 192 excluded reference moves.** These counts reflect actual calls through the current calculator with valid neutral, known-ability data, complete IVs/friendship/weight and no unsupported state. They are regression-locked, and the complete current inventories appear below.
- **All39 restored moves reviewed individually**, including source metadata and battle-script routing. All39 retain their eligibility for their ordinary connected hit in represented conditions. No additional move or mechanic was enabled in this correction.
- **AUD-01 resolved:** the offensive stat operand can now become zero after Thick Fat and negative stages. Its independent fixture now produces **1–2 HP**, replacing the former3–4. The existing defensive minimum/validation remains to prevent division by zero.
- No popup/percentage color, Battle Conditions, stat-bar, move-row, team-header, other-tab/page or save-data changes. The only production change is removing the offensive clamp in `basicDamage.js::prepareBasicDamage`. Thick Fat's existing preprocessing was inspected and remains correct and unchanged.

This is source review and representative numerical/browser verification, not an exhaustive cartridge certification. An eligible move is not a promise that every external battle state or ability activation is supported.

## AUD-01 — resolved

**Root cause:** `prepareBasicDamage` wrapped the offensive `stageStat(...)` result in `Math.max(1,...)`. Emerald's `APPLY_STAT_MOD` can legitimately produce a zero offensive operand, notably after Thick Fat's integer halving and a strongly negative SpA stage. That zero must reach the base formula; special damage still receives +2.

**Correction:** remove only the offensive minimum-one clamp. Thick Fat still truncates SpA before stage scaling. Preserve raw stat validation and the existing defensive `Math.max(1,...)` safety guard. No related stat formula, display stat, HP value or stage value was changed. The defensive guard is a safety policy for extreme helper inputs, not a claim about cartridge behavior on an invalid zero divisor.

**Independent fixture:** Lv1 Smeargle SpA5, Ice Hidden Power70 (IV order HP2/Atk2/Def2/Spe3/SpA3/SpD3), SpA−6; Normal/Thick Fat target with SpD5, SpD−6. Thick Fat floor5/2=2; stage floor2/4=0; target floor5/4=1. Base quotient0,+2=2; random1–2 HP. **Current actual:1–2 HP.** The previous3–4 was caused by replacing the zero offensive operand with1.

The earlier characterization test is now a positive cartridge-correct regression, retaining the independent expected arithmetic and asserting equality. It no longer endorses the old discrepancy.

Boundary evidence in `finalDamageAudit.test.js`:

- Raw SpA1/2/3/4/5/7, Thick Fat,−6=>zero offensive operand=>1–2 HP.
- Raw SpA8/9, Thick Fat,−6=>operand1=>3–4 HP.
- Raw SpA5 without Thick Fat,−6=>operand1=>3–4 HP.
- Critical ignores negative SpA, retains negative SpD: offense2/defense1=>base7,crit14=>11–14 HP.
- Critical ignores positive SpD instead: offense2/defense5=>base3,crit6=>5–6 HP.
- Physical zero offense still uses the existing physical minimum subtotal=>2–3 HP in its independent fixture.
- A defensive intermediate that would become0 remains guarded to1; all rolls finite. Raw defense0 is rejected. Ordinary Tower tests continue passing.

The exact low-stat target is outside generated Tower50–100 stats, but the helper arithmetic is now corrected rather than left as an open exception.
